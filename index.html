Webinar Series

Preparing for the Panini Linguistics Olympiad

The Panini Linguistics Olympiad (PLO) is India's national selection process for the International Linguistics Olympiad (IOL). In these two webinars we explain how PLO works, how to prepare for it, and solve practice problems together.

Hosted by ClearThink's PLO Ambassador, appointed by the PLO Organizing Committee at LTRC, IIIT Hyderabad.
