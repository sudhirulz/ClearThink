Research

Adaptive Random Matrices in Gaussian Bandits: Spectral Universality and Selection-Induced Outliers

Mentor and co-author: Abhishek Bhattacharjee, Abstract Math Institute · 2025 to 2026 · Domains: probability and statistics, machine learning
  • Proved that data collected adaptively by any bandit algorithm keeps the same eigenvalue distribution as independent data (the Marchenko-Pastur law), and found exactly when an algorithm's directional bias shows up as a single outlier eigenvalue
