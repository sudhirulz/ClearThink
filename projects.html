Adaptive Random Matrices in Gaussian Bandits: Spectral Universality and Selection-Induced Outliers

Domain
Mathematics (probability and statistics), machine learning; random matrix theory, bandit algorithms
Mentor
Abhishek Bhattacharjee, Abstract Math Institute (co-author)

Bandit algorithms choose which data to collect based on what they have already seen, so the data are not independent. We proved that, for every bandit algorithm, this adaptively collected data still follows the same eigenvalue distribution as independent data (the Marchenko-Pastur law). We also found the exact threshold at which an algorithm's directional bias shows up as a single outlier eigenvalue.

Baku Linguistic Landscape Field Research

Domain
Sociolinguistics, contact linguistics, linguistic landscape studies, computational linguistics, open data
Mentor
Self-initiated, with guidance from Dr Parameshwari Krishnamurthy, IIIT Hyderabad

What started as a family trip to Baku turned into a linguistics project when I began photographing shop signs and street announcements to see how Azerbaijani, Russian and English mix in everyday public life. I documented code-switching across 13 languages, including the endangered isolate Khinaliq, recorded speech from a bilingual Baku resident, and published an open corpus of 99 geotagged entries and 2,516 annotated words.

SwaraSync: Carnatic Music-Based Speech Therapy for Adolescent Stuttering

Domain
Music theory, phonetics, psychology, speech therapy
Mentor
Dr Ragasudha, speech therapist and Carnatic musician
Recognition
IRIS National Fair 2026, Translational Medical Science category

I built a web app that analyses a stammering teenager's speech, identifies their problem sounds, and prescribes Carnatic swara and rhythm exercises that target those sounds, at a tempo set from the speaker's own fluent rate. In a four-week pilot with 18 adolescents, the SwaraSync group improved more than the control group on clinician-rated stuttering severity (SSI-4, p = 0.022). Dysfluency and social anxiety also improved more, though these differences were not significant (p = 0.077 and p = 0.084).

NeuroNudge: A Personalised AI Chatbot Intervention for Adolescent Moral Reasoning

Domain
Cognitive neuroscience, behavioural psychology, human-AI interaction
Mentor
Amal Ashwin Jude, PhD researcher in neuroscience, IIT Kanpur

I designed a moral-judgement task in Python with synchronised EEG, eye-tracking and behavioural logging. After a ten-minute conversation with a personalised chatbot, adolescents made decisions faster and with more confidence, while their underlying moral judgements stayed the same. These changes were statistically significant.

LinguaQuest

Domain
Linguistics education, community building, outreach
Mentor
Self-initiated; used by the PLO organisers at IIIT Hyderabad

Few students in India know about linguistics or the Linguistics Olympiad, and practice material is scattered across the web. I built LinguaQuest to bring problems from 20+ countries, beginner guides and resources into one free platform, with a moderated feedback wall and a discussion space where students share problems, solutions and resources. It has reached 1,000+ users and is being used to grow participation in next year's PLO.

ClearThink: Detect Logical Fallacies and Build Better Arguments

Domain
Logic, argumentation, machine learning, community and social impact
Mentor
Dr Jon Hanke, Princeton University (AIMS Summer Camp)

ClearThink is a student-led initiative I founded to teach people to spot logical fallacies and build sound arguments. It is built around Busted!, a free web app with a guide to 12 common fallacies, a quiz, and a scanner that checks writing sentence by sentence using a custom fine-tuned AI model. Through ClearThink workshops on fallacies, argument building and logic puzzles, I have worked with 250+ students.
