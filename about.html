The Person Behind the Project

About the Founder

ClearThink is a community effort, but every community effort starts with someone. Here's who started this one.

Founder

Sudarshan Manikantan

IBDP Student · National-Level Debater · IOL '26

I'm an IBDP student and a national-level debater, having competed at tournaments like ICYD at the Oxford Union, Asia WSDC in Thailand, and university-run tournaments hosted by Columbia, Princeton, UPenn, and Monash. More recently I've moved into adjudicating too, judging high school tournaments like Paris WSDC and Western BP.

I stumbled into logical fallacies through debate rounds, hearing college debaters casually drop terms like "straw man" or "slippery slope." The vocabulary was new, but the ideas made total sense. So I looked into it properly, and ClearThink is the result.

I'm also into Math, Logic, and Linguistics. This project sits right where those interests meet: using AI to make something usually locked away in debate clubs a little more accessible to everyone else.
